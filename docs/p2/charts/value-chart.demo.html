The adversary's attempts

Each of these is a way to make this chart say something false, and each one must fail. Most fail by being refused. The first two fail differently and on purpose: a claim the record marks rejected is drawn, because the record repaired its body in place and the sentence on the chart is the correction — what cannot happen is drawing it with the verdict invisible. Those two are marked DRAWN, AND VISIBLE, and the run fails if either one is refused or arrives without a stamp.

These are the attacks on this chart and on the library under it. The whole chart layer’s bench — the sealed plan, the midpoint, the record on the plan, the containers, and the two branches the frozen record never reaches — is charts.test.html.
