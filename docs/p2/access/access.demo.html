Four real instruments, drawn from the frozen record: one era machine, the auction bench with THE BAND, the door bench with the drum, and the seven toll plates. Each is wrapped in its declared visual, and the text path is mounted over all of them.

Press text only and every drawing goes. Nothing else does: every crank, rocker, slider, ring and the drum are still there, still operable, and the text under each one is rebuilt the moment you move it. Tab through the page and each composite control costs one stop. On a drawing, Left and Right walk what it says, one reading at a time.
