P2 · team B6 · the toll plate

Seven middlemen, seven cuts, and no two of them measured the same way.

A middleman sits between the advertiser and the place the advertisement appears, and keeps a slice of the money. Every era wrote that slice down as a percentage. What the percentage was of changed every time. Bars in a row on one ruler would invite you to read an early percentage against a late one and conclude the cut rose. Nothing in the record says that, and this page will not print the two figures side by side to make the point either.

So there is no chart here, and no shared scale. There are thirteen small drawings, each one showing a dollar going in and a slice coming out, drawn at a size that belongs to that base and to nothing else. Two of them cannot be measured against each other. Comparing two of these figures means reading what each one is a share of first, which is the honest amount of work.
