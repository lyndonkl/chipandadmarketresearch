Google won search advertising twice.

It built a better auction. Then it paid other companies to make Google the search box people met first. The bench next door covers the first half. This one covers the second, and most readers have never heard it.

Two lanes run into one auction. One starts on Google's own page. The other starts on somebody else's and has to come through a door. Only the lower lane passes a valve, and the valve taps most of the money straight back out through the door it came in by.

The drum across the top of the instrument is the revenue share. Grab it and turn it, or tab to it and use the arrow keys. Watch what happens under your own hand.
