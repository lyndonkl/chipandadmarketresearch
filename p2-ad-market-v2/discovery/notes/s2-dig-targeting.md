# S2 dig — the addressable base: the precision the buyer's yield never justified, and the dial it gave back

**Role:** Narrative Architect (Bash), Discovery Session 2. **Facet:** the 150-year addressable base —
how targeting went from mass to segmented to individual, the response rates a buyer actually got, the
reach a buyer could address, and how *who could be targeted* changed each era. **This is a discovery
note, not an article and not an architecture.** It drills ONE facet of the unexplored quarter to the
bottom and extends the meta-spine. It does not re-mine the six S1 threads or repeat the S2 sweeps.

**Provenance discipline (carried from the dossier).** Records *provenance, not verification.* **No claim
here has been verified. Human verification status: none.** Every finding traces to a graph path + claim
id(s). Grades (A/B/C), hedges and intervals are the sources' own and preserved. `null`-grade clause
fragments carry no number; numbers were re-pulled from the backing `e#-` claim via `claim.py`. `tu:era:*`
clauses are **synthesis (SECONDARY)**. Verdicts cross-checked inline via `claim.py`: every featured id
reads `confirmed` or `adjusted` (usable), and **none reads `rejected`** (`e7-medium-003` not used). Named
living parties (Google, Meta, Apple) are **surfaced, not adjudicated.**

**What this extends, not repeats.** `s2-sweep-graph` built the *reach ladder* (title→household→ZIP→query
→person→opt-out). `s2-sweep-hub` built the *direct-response buyer's meter* (keyed action, cost per order).
Those are the REACH and the METER axes. This dig drills the two axes those sweeps flagged but did not open.
First, the **YIELD** the buyer actually got from all that precision (the 2% response, the unread base).
Second, the **DIAL** — who chose the target — which the buyer held for 150 years and surrendered in era 7.

## 0. The through-line (one line)

**Targeting got ~279,000× finer and the buyer's response never moved. So in the end the buyer stopped
choosing the target at all. It handed the one lever it had held for 150 years to the party that already
owns the count and the price.**

The addressable base was engineered all the way down to the individual. By 1975 direct mail could reach
71.1M US households one at a time, "279,000× finer than the average unit spot television could sell"
(`e3-targeting-005 A`). Precision was sold as the cure for waste. But the *yield* it was meant to fix
stayed on the floor. Two responses per 100 was a *successful* direct-mail campaign (`e4-targeting-004 B`),
and only ~4% of even delivered mail was ever read (`e5-targeting-005 B`). For 150 years the buyer's answer
to that gap was to choose a finer target — sort titles by cost per reply, rent the list, pick the ZIP
cluster, bid the keyword. The era-7 answer *reverses* it. By 2025, 71% of Google advertisers ran
Performance Max — "an advertiser who no longer picks keywords" who "hands a conversion goal to the seller's
model" (`e7-buyers-003 C`; `tu:era:7:field:BUYERS`, synthesis). The buyer gave the dial back.

**How it connects to the meta-spine.** S1: *the independent number was the anomaly — by default the judged
seller owns the count and the price.* This axis adds a **third thing the seller captured: the targeting
decision.** The direct-response buyer invented the independent number — the keyed coupon, cost per reply,
cost per order — precisely to escape the seller's audience count. On the targeting axis, its century-long
project ends with the seller's model choosing the audience, running the auction, counting the conversion
and reporting the result. **The buyer's independent hand on the dial was the anomaly.** This *sharpens* the
spine with a new capture on the demand side, rather than repeating it.

## 2. The spine (extract-thread-spine / ABT)

**AND** for 150 years the buyer's targeting got relentlessly finer. It ran from 5,778 selectable titles
(1869) to 71.1M households addressable one at a time, "279,000× finer than spot TV" (1975), and on to the
query and then the person. At every step, *the buyer chose the target* `[e1-targeting-001 B,
e3-targeting-005 A, e5-targeting-004 A]`.
**BUT** the yield that precision was sold to fix never moved: 2 of 100 was a *successful* campaign (missed
98), and only ~4% of even delivered mail was ever read `[e4-targeting-004 B, e5-targeting-005 B]`.
**THEREFORE** the buyer's answer, in the end, was not a finer target but to *stop choosing*. By 2025, 71%
of Google advertisers ran Performance Max — "an advertiser who no longer picks keywords" who hands a
conversion goal to the seller's model. The buyer surrendered the one lever it had held for 150 years. It
handed that lever to the party that already owns the count and the price `[e7-buyers-003 C,
tu:era:7:field:BUYERS synthesis]`.

**Designing principle:** *follow one thing — the buyer's hand on the targeting dial — from the newspaper
directory to the seller's model.* It **cuts** (excludes ≥3): the sell-side concentration story (Thread 3),
the commission/take-rate story (Thread 2), and the reach ladder read on its own (the sweeps' person→query
→person). This spine is about *who decides* and *what the deciding bought*, not who counts or how fine.

**Form (six-question gate, targeting regime as system subject).** The verdict is **EXPLANATORY NARRATIVE,
rung 2** — an action line with no single transformation, consistent with the dossier's per-thread pattern.

- Q1 continuous entity — **YES** (the addressable base / targeting regime, 1869→2026).
- Q2 documented want — **YES**, collective (measured response: keyed coupon → cost per order → pay-per-action).
- Q3 dated ruptures — **YES** (1904, 1963, 1974, 2001, 2007, 2021, 2025).
- Q4 ≥3 renderable moments — **YES**.
- Q5 point of insight — **ABSENT** (the surrender is gradual; no dated self-revelation).
- Q6 resolution — **ABSENT** (ongoing; PMax adoption rising, cookie deprecation in flux).

The two ABSENTs are load-bearing; all-slots-filled would be the fabrication signature. **Structure: spiral
over chronology** — re-enter one question (*who holds the dial, and what does the yield justify?*) at the
title, the daypart, the ZIP cluster, the query, the model. **Runner-up: dual-profile** on the denominator
"the individually addressable household" — direct mail's 71.1M in 1975 vs the era-7 person fed to the model.

## 3. Systemic protagonist (POSIWID sheet) — the targeting regime

- **Reliably converts:** *the buyer's demand for less waste* into *a finer and finer sorting of who can be
  addressed* — never into a higher response. Reach fell from "one title" to "one household" to "one query"
  (`e1-targeting-001`→`e3-targeting-005`→`e5-targeting-004`); the working yield stayed ~2% (`e4-targeting-004`).
- **Stated purpose (revealed by product):** precision = the buyer's control over waste (rank titles by cost
  per reply, `tu:era:1`; "which streets to buy," `tu:era:4`).
- **Revealed function (two regimes).** In the direct-mail era, reach climbed toward the individual while the
  buyer's response stayed floored at ~2% (`e4-targeting-004`). In the platform era, reach climbed again
  while the buyer's *targeting decision itself* migrated to the seller's model (Performance Max 71%,
  `e7-buyers-003`; Advantage+ $60B run rate, `e7-targeting-005`). Precision sold as control delivered, at
  the end, the buyer's loss of control.
- **The gap (dramatic engine):** the buyer was sold precision as *its* instrument against the seller's
  number; the terminal state is the buyer handing the instrument to the seller.
- **Mandatory residual (blocks the malign-optimiser read).** The buyer's own **meter won** even as its
  **dial** was surrendered. Pay-per-action rose to **57% of internet revenue by 2008** (`e6-buyers-008 A`),
  so the direct-response buyer's pricing model became the market default. Two things moved in opposite
  directions: the meter went the buyer's way, the dial went the seller's. A pure "seller captured
  everything" story cannot explain why the market ended up priced on the buyer's preferred metric.
- **Constraint, not villain (opposition-web → constraint inventory).** The binding constraint on yield was
  never addressability — it was **countability**. `tu:era:2:field:TARGETING`: "the finest unit anyone could
  actually price was a daypart in a market for a class of household; **nothing narrower was purchasable,
  because nothing narrower was countable**" (anchored `e2-targeting-004 B`, 1→32 interview sets/day). And
  most of the addressed never respond (2% success; ~96% of mail unread). Targeting's ceiling was always
  *measurement's* ceiling — the tie straight into Thread 1. The **state** appears as enabler (ZIP 1963,
  `e3-targeting-004 A`) and re-separator (ATT 2021, `e7-targeting-002 B`), consistent with Thread 3. No
  named party is cast as antagonist.

## 4. How it complicates / sharpens the sweeps and the meta-spine

1. **Sharpens the sweeps' "person→query→person reversion."** The era-7 endpoint is **not** "the person
   returns as a target the buyer picks." The corpus author's own framing is that the person becomes a
   *signal fed to the seller's model*, and "the advertiser stopped choosing the audience"
   (`tu:era:7:field:TARGETING`, synthesis; `e7-buyers-003`). So the reach ladder does not end at "person."
   It ends at *targeting leaving the buyer's hands.* **(My read, marked hypothesis:** the graph supports the
   PMax-adoption number and the "seller's model" clauses; the "surrender of the dial" frame is mine,
   grounded in them.)
2. **Complements the hub sweep's direct-response-buyer thread.** That sweep found the buyer's METER (pay by
   keyed action) won. This dig adds that the buyer's DIAL (choose the target) was surrendered. The two
   moving in opposite directions is the residual that keeps both honest.
3. **Adds the YIELD axis the reach ladder never carried.** The addressable base and the *responsive* base
   were separated by ~2 orders of magnitude the whole direct era. 71.1M addressable one-at-a-time sat
   against a 2% response and a ~4%-read rate. Precision was real; the yield it bought was not.
4. **Extends the meta-spine by one capture.** The judged seller owns the count (T1) and the price (T5). On
   the targeting axis, by era 7 the seller's model also owns the *decision.* *The buyer's independent hand
   on the dial was the anomaly.*

## 6. Agency reading (required) and grade cautions

**Agency reading.** Strongest agency reading: *platforms built Performance Max / Advantage+ to seize the
targeting decision and lock buyers into a seller-run regime.* **Considered, not adopted as the spine.** Two
reasons from the corpus. First, the yield constraint was **structural and pre-platform** — the buyer's
response had been floored by countability and non-response for a century (2% in era 4; ~96% of mail unread)
before any platform existed. Automation is sold as the buyer's escape from a yield problem it never solved
by choosing. Second, the **residual**: adoption is buyer-*pulled* — 71% of advertisers *opt in* to
Performance Max (`e7-buyers-003`), and the buyer's own pay-per-action meter became the default
(`e6-buyers-008 A`). Handing the choice to a model that optimises measured response continues the
direct-response buyer's 150-year logic; it is not only a seller capture. Structural + buyer-pull, consistent
with the meta-spine. **But surfaced, not softened:** the buyer's surrender lands in the one regime where the
seller already owns the count and the price, so the outcome favours the seller regardless of intent. Named
parties (Google, Meta, Apple) are surfaced, not adjudicated.

**Grade cautions / representativeness.**
- Load-bearing anchors are hard. **A6 `e3-targeting-005` A** (71.1M, 279,000×) and **B2 `e4-targeting-004` B**
  (2% response) are the spine's two pillars; both `confirmed`/adjusted.
- **A2 (`e1-targeting-002 B`)** is a claim about REACH, not RESPONSE. Keep the two axes distinct; the
  addressable individual (1904, in the mail) is not a responding individual.
- **B3 `e5-targeting-005 B`**: the ~4%-read figure is derived in the claim's own base note. Ship it with the
  "pieces read ≠ pieces mailed" guard the claim itself carries.
- **C3 `e7-buyers-003` is grade C** — the whole era-7 surrender endpoint is the softest link. It is
  corroborated only directionally by `e7-targeting-005 B` (run rate). Flag on every use.
- **Anachronism:** "targeting," "precision," "yield," "conversion," "the algorithmic buyer" are partly
  modern frames. Era 1 was "choosing a publication, choosing a place, **not** choosing a person"
  (`tu:era:1:field:TARGETING`).
