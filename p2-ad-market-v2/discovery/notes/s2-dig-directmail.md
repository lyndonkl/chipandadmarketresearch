## 0. The through-line

**"What counts as advertising" is the market's oldest unsettled number — and direct mail is the channel it
was settled against.** The denominator that every share statistic divides into was never a fact about the
world; it was always a choice of basis, and the corpus wires that choice as a countable, recurring
structural fact. Direct mail is the load-bearing witness. For ~70 years (1935–2007) it was the
**second-largest or the single largest named medium** in the one continuous series (Coen/McCann). Yet it is
written out of the "US ad market" total **at least eight times** across the corpus, and by 2025 the headline
basis (MAGNA media-owner revenue, $398B) **excludes it entirely.** The same booking convention that makes
direct mail "the biggest medium" on one basis makes it vanish on another. So "TV conquered advertising,"
"search ate the market," and "advertising is 1.32% of GDP, the lowest since the war" are each **claims about
a basis, not facts about the world.** The basis moved in the direction that flattered whoever was counting.

**Meta-spine connection, one line:** *the independent number was the anomaly* (S1: who counts / prices /
concentrates) has a **denominator twin — the independent denominator was also the anomaly.** The total
itself is owned by whoever draws its boundary, and the boundary was redrawn era by era to keep the
response-priced, un-agencied channels no compiler franchises (direct mail, trade promotion, catalogue,
lead-gen) out of frame. The POSIWID gap, one layer below the number: at the **base**.

## 1. Frame lock (the frame is the thread)

- **UNIT:** direct mail's share of *the US advertising total*.
- **DENOMINATOR:** *the US advertising total* — and this is exactly the unstable quantity. **The denominator
  is the protagonist.** Rejected alternative: fixing one basis (e.g. Coen) and reading direct mail's share
  off it — rejected because that hides the war; the war *is* the disagreement between bases.
- **WINDOW:** 1914 (first benchmark that carries direct-response money) → 2025 (MAGNA media-owner rail).
  Rejected alt: 1935–2007 (the clean Coen window) — rejected because the story's hinge is precisely the
  2008→2025 basis switch that drops direct mail.
- **BOUNDARY:** the population is *media through which paid persuasion is bought*. Who drew the line is the
  whole question — Coen/McCann drew it one way (direct mail in, biggest line), MAGNA/IAB draw it another
  (media-owner revenue only, direct mail out).
- **Starting hypothesis (dated 2026-08-08, before drilling):** "direct mail is a big medium the headline
  totals ignore; the omission understates it." **Diff after drilling in §7.**

## 3. The mechanism — what entered and left the base, era by era (the denominator war proper)

The arc above is one channel. The **war** is the general fact the corpus wires ~50 times: the "US ad market"
total is a boundary choice, and the boundary excludes different money in different eras. Direct mail is the
recurring casualty; it is not the only one.

**(a) The exclusion, eight times, graph-wired.** `MATCH (a:L0)-[EXCLUDES|EXCLUDED]->(b:L0)` where `b` is
direct mail returns **eight self-annotated edges**: `e2-scale-010`, `e3-buyers-003`, `e5-buyers-010`,
`e7-buyers-006`, `e7-scale-001`, `e7-scale-006`, `e7-scale-008`, `e7-scale-009`. The corpus author installed
the "do-not-read-this-total-as-the-market" guard eight separate times.

- **First instance, 1949:** "National brand advertising, **excluding direct mail**, was about $2,234M in
  1949 — roughly 43 percent of all US advertising" `[e2-scale-010 C]`. Direct mail (14.5%, the #2 medium)
  is subtracted *before* the national-brand total is struck.

**(b) Money that never enters the base at all.** "Trade promotion took **44.9%** of packaged-goods
marketing-communications budgets by 1992 … **money that never appears in the measured-media advertising
total**" `[e4-buyers-004 B]`. By 1992 nearly half of CPG persuasion spend is outside the denominator by
definition — a second channel-sized hole, alongside direct mail.

**(c) The platform-era rail halves the base by definition.** The 2025 headline is the MAGNA media-owner
rail: **$398B** ("digital pure players $294B + traditional media owners $104B, which sums exactly, so direct
mail is out") `[e7-scale-006 C, method]`. Against it the corpus sets the IRS Statistics-of-Income
corporate-advertising-deduction rail: **$473,153M for 2022 vs MAGNA's $325,000M** — a **$148B wedge, 45.6%
of the MAGNA base, "overwhelmingly direct-response and retail money"** `[e7-scale-006 C, method]`.
Classified/directory money is folded to **$9.95B (2.5%)** and lead-gen ($4.5–6B) sits with direct response,
not here `[e7-scale-008 C]`. **The instruction the corpus gives itself is the thread's climax line:**
*"Any chart of this split must name its rail in the axis label, not the footnote."*

**(d) Same quantity, two irreconcilable bases — the war made explicit (three named conflicts).**
- Radio, 1932: **3.9%** on Coen vs **~11%** on a narrower national-media base — "**the conflict is not
  resolved**" `[e2-medium-003 B]`.
- Internet, 2000: **2.6% / 3.3% / 3.7%** — "the numerator and the denominator come from different series …
  **Basis must be declared, not blended**" `[e5-medium-004 C]`.
- US total, 1900: **$450M (Coen) vs $542M (Printers' Ink)** — "disagree by 20 percent and are **both
  carried**" `[e1-scale-004 B]`.

Every one of these is a "share" that a casual reader treats as a fact and the corpus flags as a basis.

## 4. The systemic protagonist — POSIWID on the denominator

The protagonist is not a person and not a firm. It is **the instrument that defines "advertising"** — the
Coen/McCann series (curated by Galbi) at one end, the MAGNA/IAB media-owner rail at the other.

- **Stated purpose (quoted function):** measure the US advertising market — produce "the" total.
- **Revealed function (POSIWID):** *reliably converts a broad, stable commercial-persuasion economy into a
  single share statistic whose basis is never printed on the axis*, systematically dropping the
  response-priced, un-agencied, self-serve channels that no compiler franchises.
- **The gap (the engine):** the total presents as a fact about the market; it is a fact about the counter's
  franchise. Direct mail is biggest **or** invisible depending only on who drew the boundary.

**Two regimes (required).**
1. **Coen/McCann regime (1935–2007):** direct mail is *in*, and is the biggest or #2 line every year (§2).
   Yet even here the base is gamed two ways: "national brand" totals **subtract** direct mail
   (`e2-scale-010`), and trade promotion never enters (`e4-buyers-004`).
2. **Media-owner regime (2008–2025):** the base **drops** direct mail wholesale and folds
   classified/directory/catalogue/lead-gen into "Other" or out (`e7-scale-001/006/008`). The denominator is
   ~$148B smaller than the IRS rail *by definition*.

**Mandatory residual (blocks the "counter always flatters itself" reading).** The distortion is **not
one-directional**, so the denominator is not a clean malign optimiser:
- In the Coen series direct mail is **booked as entirely national** (`tu:era:4:field:SCALE`;
  `e5-buyers-010` subtracts it from National). That **inflates** the national-brand pool and distorts the
  national/local split in the *opposite* direction to an exclusion.
- The 2025 exclusion in `e7-scale-006` is introduced as a **bug-fix**: all four money pools are redefined
  ex-direct-mail "to match," repairing a residual-depression error where direct mail had been "excluded from
  the denominator but present in two subtracted pools." Here dropping direct mail is the *honest* move.

So every boundary choice has a defensible reason and an indefensible effect. **The closest thing to a human
protagonist is the corpus author/verifier** — the one who keeps writing the guard ("name its rail in the
axis label") and computing the wedge. This is Thread 1's shape (*who audits the counter?*) at the base
layer: *who audits the denominator?*

## 5. Opposition — a constraint, not a villain (no corners)

There is no antagonist and no four-corner web here; an explanatory spine has constraints. The binding
constraint is **the economics of compilation**: no single compiler has ever held franchise over the
response-priced, un-agencied channels, so they fall out of whatever total that compiler can build. Coen
books direct mail from McCann; MAGNA counts *media-owner revenue* and a direct mailer sells no inventory;
the IAB counts *internet* revenue and buckets directories as "Other." The exclusion is **structural — each
instrument counts what it can franchise — not conspiratorial.** The corpus renders this as the ~50-edge
Family A of the opposition sweep (8 direct-mail exclusions + the unresolved same-quantity conflicts).
Caveat inherited from the opp sweep: these are **self-edges** — author caveats installed inside single
claims, not independent sources warring. State that on handoff.

**Agency reading (required).** Strongest agency reading: *compilers deliberately exclude direct mail to
inflate the medium they franchise.* **Considered, not adopted.** The exclusion is structural — a mailer is
not a media owner, and direct mail is USPS/DMA-measured, outside every media-owner rail. The direction of
distortion is **inconsistent**: booked-as-national inflates, the `e7-scale-006` exclusion is a fix.
Structural, not agentic — consistent with the meta-spine. Named parties surfaced, not adjudicated.

## 6. How it connects to — and complicates — the meta-spine

**Extends it.** *The independent number was the anomaly* → *the independent denominator was also the
anomaly.* Same POSIWID gap, one layer down: the total is owned by whoever draws its boundary, and the
boundary is redrawn to keep the un-franchised channels out. Who *counts* (Thread 1) and *what is in the
base* (this facet) are the same structure at the measurement and the basis layers.

**Complicates Thread 6 (capture, not expansion) at its headline number.** Thread 6's spine is "advertising
fell from 3.0% of GDP (1922) to ~1.32% (2025), the lowest since WWII." The corpus's own hedge:
**"the ratio's fall is partly real and partly a change in what 'advertising' counts"** `[e7-scale-002 C,
method]`. The single biggest thing that stopped being counted between the two ends is **direct mail — 16.8%
of the total at its 1938 peak, 21.5% as late as 2007, zero in the 2025 basis.** The trough is understated by
exactly the channel the basis dropped. So the denominator war is not a footnote to the capture thread; it is
a **load-bearing correction to its load-bearing number.** (Non-commensurability caveat is already
non-removable across the corpus; this facet says *what specifically* drives the incommensurability.)

**Complicates every "medium killed medium" story.** No medium's "share" is a fact: radio 1932 (3.9 vs 11),
internet 2000 (2.6 vs 3.7), the dot-com "peak" all move when the basis moves. "TV conquered advertising" and
"search ate the market" are basis artefacts before they are market facts.

## 7. DEAD / CONTRADICTED column (must reach the reader)

- **"US advertising is ~1.32% of GDP, the lowest since WWII"** — **CONTESTED / non-commensurable.** The 2025
  basis (MAGNA media-owner) excludes direct mail; the 1922/1944 Coen basis included it (16.8% at peak).
  "Partly real and partly a change in what advertising counts" `[e7-scale-002 C]`. Ship the caveat.
- **"Direct mail is a minor / dying channel"** — **CONTRADICTED.** Largest single line in Coen's taxonomy
  through 2007 ($60,225M, 21.5%) `[e6-medium-001 B]`; largest single national medium in 1975 `[e3-medium-006 B]`.
- **"There is a single US ad-market total"** — **CONTRADICTED at the origin.** 1900 = $450M vs $542M, 20%
  apart, both carried `[e1-scale-004 B]`; 2025 = $398B (MAGNA) vs $473B IRS-deduction rail, a $148B wedge
  `[e7-scale-006 C]`.
- **"Radio / internet / TV share is a published fact"** — **CONTRADICTED.** Radio 1932 3.9 vs 11,
  "conflict not resolved" `[e2-medium-003 B]`; internet 2000 2.6/3.3/3.7, "basis must be declared, not
  blended" `[e5-medium-004 C]`.
- **"Measured-media advertising = what advertisers spend to persuade"** — **CONTRADICTED.** Trade promotion
  alone was 44.9% of CPG marcom by 1992, never in the total `[e4-buyers-004 B]`.

**Hypothesis diff.** Started at *"direct mail is a big medium the headline totals ignore; the omission
understates it."* Ended at something sharper and two-directional: **the denominator itself is the anomaly —
the total is a franchise boundary, not a fact, and direct mail is the channel that proves it because the
same booking convention makes it biggest or invisible.** The omission does **not** simply "understate": in
the Coen era direct mail is *over*-booked as entirely national (inflating the national pool), and in 2025 its
exclusion is a deliberate consistency fix. The thread is not "the totals miss a big channel" (a completeness
complaint) but "**every share figure is a claim about a basis**" (a structural finding). The residual — the
exclusion cutting both ways — keeps it from collapsing into a malign-optimiser story.
