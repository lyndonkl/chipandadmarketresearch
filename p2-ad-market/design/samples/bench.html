Meter Thread 1 · Who counted, and who paid the counter

Somebody has to count the audience before anybody can price it.

For eighty years that somebody was not the seller. The top half of this strip names the counting instrument of the moment. The bottom half hangs the payer below the line. The deeper the block, the further the payer sat from the seller of the advertising. Watch the profile come back up at the end.

Same viewing. Two instruments. Two prices.

Read the bottom profile as one shape. In 1914 the counter moves as far from the seller as it ever gets: the Audit Bureau of Circulations was funded by publishers and advertisers together. It wanders for eighty years — an advertisers' club, then a rival medium paying to prove the first count wrong, then a private firm selling the count to both sides.

Then it collapses. From 1994 the ad server is the count, and the ad server belongs to the seller. Google recorded the impression, ran the auction, decided which click was valid and billed against its own log. The first industry standard for counting a click was not published until 2009, after the era it was needed in.

Tolerance How this page draws what it does not know

When the range is wider than the middle value, there is no middle value.

Sixty per cent is the cut. If a claim's 80% interval is wider than 60% of its central figure, this page will not draw a dot. It draws the span and nothing else. That is not a style rule. The renderer throws an error if you ask it for a point.

The four registers
Width

A ribbon's thickness is its 80% interval. Thin means the record is tight. Fat means it is not.

Texture

Source grade, coded twice. A solid: a primary record. B ruled: a published compiler. C hatched: our own construction or a reconstruction. In this sample: A 5, B 42, C 16.

Tremor

Reserved for the widest claims only. The mark refuses to settle inside its own interval. Turn motion off and the instability is printed as a number instead.

Stipple

Documented absence. Never a fill, never white space. If a stretch of years has no source, it gets a texture and a name.

Four pools of money. The record can place only one of them.
