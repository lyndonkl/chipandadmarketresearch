The wedge at 1980

1980 is the only year both compilers publish a total. They are 23.4% apart, and they are not measuring the same thing. Coen counts what advertisers were billed at list prices, across eleven media. MAGNA counts what media owners took in, across eight. Splicing them would invent a $12,549m fall that nobody observed.

About 69% of the gap is scope — which things got counted at all. Only 7.2 points of it is the price basis. So this is mostly a difference of definition, not of dollars.

The block with no rail

Coen stops in 2007. In this file the next reading is a single MAGNA point in 2018, on a different basis. Ten years — 2008 to 2017 — carry no reading at all, and 2019 and 2020 carry none either. That is drawn as a block with a name, not as whitespace and not as a line joining 2007 to 2018.

The apparent collapse after 2007 is a phantom. It is the record ending, not the market shrinking.

The nine sleepers

Thread 1 · The Counter's Ledger

Who counted · who paid them

Audited circulation arrives in 1914. From then on the market ran on one rule: somebody other than the seller counted the audience. The track below shows how far the counter's paymaster sat from the seller. It has three steps and they are in order — the seller paid, both sides paid, the buyer or a rival paid. Eighty years after 1914, the step falls to the bottom and stays there.

The Assay Mark

How a number is drawn

A mark's shape tells you how sure the number is, and the rule is arithmetic rather than taste. Divide the width of the 80% range by the middle value. Above 0.6, we draw the span and no middle mark at all — because there is nothing solid to put a dot on. Here is the rule, and here is what it produces.

A claim whose range is wider than its middle

“National brand advertising was about 22 percent of total US advertising outlay at the 1914 benchmark.” Range 11% to 46%. Width divided by middle is 1.59, so the mark is a span and nothing else. Drawing a dot at 22 would be the most dishonest thing on this page.

The rule, printed

width ÷ middle > 0.6span only, no middle mark
0.2 to 0.6span with a middle tick
below 0.2a point with whiskers

Three shapes, one boundary. After about the fourth mark you can read the rule off the picture.

Three species, three real claims

The stamp — grade rides stroke weight, not colour

Grade is ordinal, so it rides an ordered channel: weight and terminal. Grade A is heavy with a hard square cap and reads as stamped. Grade B is lighter with a round cap. Grade C is lightest and dashed. No colour is spent on grade at all — colour is doing custody work everywhere else on this page, and it cannot do two jobs.

Read the three together and you get the thesis in one line. The seller's own delivery log is grade A. The audience drop it repriced is grade B. How much of it was fraud is grade C, and nobody could pin it down.

The Unranked Field

Era 7 · four money pools

Four quantities. Three of them cannot be put in order, and no amount of drawing will change that. So they are not stacked, not sorted and not listed. All three sit on one line, on one shared axis, and they are told apart by texture — because texture has no order either.

Shrink the ranges and try to buy a ranking

This dial shrinks every range toward its own middle by the same fraction. It is a test of the ranking, not a new reading — the published 80% ranges stay drawn behind as ghosts. Pull it down as far as you like.

3 of 3 pairs cannot be ranked. National brand, direct response and local retail all overlap each other at the published widths.

Local retail separates from the other two once you cut the ranges to about two-thirds. National brand and direct response stay tangled all the way down to about 7%. You cannot buy that ranking.

No reading — ranges overlap

Largest: no answer. Smallest: classified.

The three names sit to the left of the terminal each one points at. If two would overlap, the later one lifts a row. That is label packing, not rank.

The null is a named, styled, repeated state in this design — the same block appears wherever the evidence honestly returns nothing. That is what stops it reading as a bug.

Direct response gets a middle tick and the other two do not. That is the width rule doing its job: its range is 0.46 of its middle, theirs are 0.60 and 0.68. It is not a ranking.

Honest reductions

On a 390px phone

The rail board survives. It is the honesty layer and it is the last thing that may ever be dropped — but it stops being sticky, because a sticky strip you also have to drag sideways is a failed control. In this mockup the wide charts scroll sideways inside their own frames; the page body never scrolls sideways.

In the real build the reading becomes one rail at a time, with a swipe between rails, and the 1980 wedge becomes an explicit compare state rather than a default. Two overlapping ribbons at 390px are illegible; animating the move from one rail to the other while the wedge paints in teaches the gap better than showing both at once.

The unranked field rotates so the share axis runs vertically — the one place a vertical axis is safe, because all three bars share it and none is above another. The instrument-swap readout moves from the right column to a sticky bottom bar, next to the thumb rather than the gaze path.

What is genuinely lost: reading the whole 186-year axis in one fixation. That is a real cost and no layout recovers it.

Reduced motion

Not a shrug. Every motion verb has a static replacement that carries the same information: sweep renders complete with thicker end caps, so terminations stay emphatic; tremor becomes a permanently drawn range band plus the numeric readout; settle becomes instant positioning with the previous value held as a ghost; the stamp becomes a short fade.

The signature interaction survives untouched, because its emotional payload is an absence of motion. The dots do not move in either mode.
